About Arijit Singh

Arijit Singh is a famous Indian playback singer known for his soulful voice and emotional songs. He has sung many popular songs in Hindi and other Indian languages.

Achievements

  • One of India's most popular playback singers.
  • Winner of several prestigious music awards.
  • Known for romantic, soulful and melodious songs.
  • Has performed songs in multiple Indian languages.
